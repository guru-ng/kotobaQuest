# Data sources & licences

KotobaQuest's bulk content in `public/data/` is generated by
`scripts/build-content.ts` from the open datasets below. Run
`scripts/fetch-sources.sh` to check them out, then regenerate and validate:

```sh
scripts/fetch-sources.sh
npx tsx scripts/build-content.ts
npx tsx scripts/validate-content.ts
```

| Source | Used for | Licence |

| [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) | Photographs used as Wordle picture clues | [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) |

## What this means

- The generated files in `public/data/` are **adaptations** of CC BY-SA
  material and are therefore shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. ShareAlike applies
  to that data, not to this app's source code.
- Attribution is shown in-app under *Data sources & licences* in the footer.
- WaniKani-derived fields present in `kanji-data` are **deliberately excluded**:
  that content is proprietary. `scripts/validate-content.ts` asserts none leak in.

## Known gaps

- **Example sentences.** Generated vocabulary has no example sentence. Tatoeba
  (CC BY 2.0 FR) is the obvious source; it needs per-sentence author credit.
- **Nepali.** Hand-written, and only on the curated entries. No usable
  Japanese–Nepali parallel corpus exists, and machine-translated Nepali is not
  good enough to ship for a learning app.
- **Picture clues.** The 26 curated Wordle words hotlink `images.unsplash.com`
  without naming the photographer. Using the Unsplash API would make
  per-photographer credit and `?utm_source=` links mandatory; self-hosting the
  images is the simpler fix. Generated words have no photo and fall back to a
  kanji card.
- **JLPT levels are unofficial.** The organisers stopped publishing word lists
  after the 2010 revision; Waller's lists are the de-facto standard estimate.
- **衷** is omitted: its KanjiVG file has 9 stroke paths for a 10-stroke kanji.
